About Us

Rooted in LaSalle. Ready for Essex County Weather.

From bitter January winds pushing down off the Detroit River to sticky lake-effect humidity rolling in from Lake Erie and Lake St. Clair, homes across LaSalle and Essex County earn their comfort the hard way. We are the local HVAC team making sure yours does it efficiently.

Our Story

From a Malden Road Shop to Essex County's Trusted HVAC Team

LaSalle Heating & Cooling grew up on Malden Road, between the older postwar bungalows near the riverfront and the newer subdivisions stretching out toward Amherstburg and Tecumseh. What started as a small local operation is now the crew families across the region call when the heat quits or the AC won't keep up.

We started small, taking on furnace repairs for families in LaSalle and Windsor, and earned a reputation for showing up on the coldest nights and getting the job done right. Word travels fast in a tight community like ours, and before long those repair calls grew into full installations, ductless upgrades, heat pump conversions, fireplace work, and duct cleaning across LaSalle, Tecumseh, Amherstburg, Lakeshore, Belle River, Leamington, and the rest of Essex County.

Essex County's weather taught us the rest of what we know. January cold snaps that drop straight down from Michigan, humid July air that sits heavy off the lakes, older homes near Front Road with ductwork that has collected decades of dust, and growing families adding ductless cooling to a finished basement, every one of those situations calls for a different answer. We have spent years learning what actually works in houses like yours.

Today we cover it all: furnace and air conditioner installation and repair, energy-efficient heat pumps, ductless mini-splits, thorough duct cleaning, fireplace installation, and smart-thermostat upgrades. The services have grown. The standard has not: real diagnostics, clean workmanship, and standing behind every job we touch.

Our Commitment

What Essex County Homeowners Can Count On

We do not believe in cookie-cutter HVAC. A drafty 1960s bungalow near the Detroit River, a two-storey in a newer LaSalle subdivision, and a waterfront home out toward Lakeshore all have different comfort needs. We size every furnace, air conditioner, and heat pump to the house it is actually in, with proper load calculations rather than a one-size-fits-all template.

That means code-compliant fireplace installs, ductless systems sized for the rooms they serve, and duct cleaning that lifts years of buildup out of older Essex County ductwork. It also means being there when it counts, on the coldest winter night and during a July heat wave alike. Honest pricing, clear communication, and workmanship we are willing to put our name on, every time.
